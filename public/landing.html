FAQ

Common Questions

How does payment work at the event?
After recording, guests see a QRIS QR code on the kiosk screen. They scan it with GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, LinkAja, or any QRIS-enabled mobile banking app (BCA, Mandiri, BRI, etc.). Payment is confirmed instantly, and the media is processed automatically.
What payment methods do you accept?
All QRIS-enabled payment methods: GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, LinkAja, and all major Indonesian mobile banking apps (BCA Mobile, BRImo, Mandiri Livin', BNI Mobile, etc.).
What is your refund policy?
Refunds are evaluated case-by-case. Full refunds are guaranteed for technical failures on our side (failed delivery, kiosk malfunction, duplicate payment). See our Refund Policy for complete details.
How do guests receive their video & photo?
After payment, our server renders the video and photo with custom overlays and sends them directly to the guest's WhatsApp number and Email — typically within 60 seconds. No app download required.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We never store credit card or banking data. All QRIS payments go directly through regulated payment infrastructure. Our systems run on HTTPS/SSL, and personal data is retained only as long as necessary (30 days for media, 90 days for contact info).
